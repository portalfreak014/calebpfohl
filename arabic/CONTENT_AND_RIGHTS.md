# Content and Rights Statement

## Purpose

This Arabic study project provides original software features and independently organized vocabulary-study data, including glossary, flashcard, matching, quiz, and progress-tracking functionality.

## What the project includes

- Individual Arabic words, short expressions, grammatical forms, and English glosses
- Original organization, normalization, and formatting of vocabulary data
- Original website code, interface design, study tools, and documentation

Individual words and short factual translations are generally not protected by copyright on their own. This project uses vocabulary as study data rather than reproducing textbook pages, lesson text, explanations, exercises, layouts, illustrations, or audio.

## What the project does not host

The deployed site and this repository are intended not to host or distribute source PDFs, page scans, screenshots, textbook images, audio from course materials, or other copyrighted course files. The project also does not reproduce textbook pages or their original presentation.

The project may include independently entered vocabulary lists derived from study and reference materials. Source materials remain subject to the rights and terms of their respective copyright holders.
