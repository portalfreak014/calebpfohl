# Voluntary Support Policy

## Free and open access

This Arabic study project is provided as a free study resource. All publicly available project features and content are available without payment.

## Optional support

The project may include an optional external donation or support link, such as Buy Me a Coffee or Ko-fi, to help with maintenance and future development.

Donations are voluntary. They do not purchase access to the project, unlock features or content, create donor-only benefits, or provide preferential treatment.

## Non-commercial intent

The project is maintained as a non-commercial educational resource. It does not sell access to its study tools or vocabulary content, run paid subscriptions, place advertising, or offer paid services through the site.

## Hosting-program eligibility

A voluntary donation link does not change the project's open-source license status. However, hosting providers may apply their own eligibility rules to open-source or nonprofit programs. The maintainer will follow the applicable provider policies and seek clarification when necessary.
