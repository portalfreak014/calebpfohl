Ancient Egyptian · beginner starter

Learn Egyptian hieroglyphs

A small foundation for learning signs—not an English-to-hieroglyph translator. Ancient Egyptian is a different language from Arabic.

Your starting path

  1. Recognize the eight starter signs in the reference.
  2. Use flashcards to recall each sign's transliteration.
  3. Test sign recognition with the multiple-choice quiz.

What are you learning?

Uniliteral signs represent a single sound. The picture and its sound value are different things: the owl sign, for example, can write m.

This starter covers only a small selection, not the complete writing system or Egyptian grammar. Transliteration is a written convention, not a recording of ancient pronunciation.

If signs appear as empty boxes, your device may need an Egyptian Hieroglyphs font such as Noto Sans Egyptian Hieroglyphs. Sign descriptions and transliterations remain available.
