A foundation, not a translator

Ancient Egyptian is a different language from Arabic. Single-sound (uniliteral) signs are only one part of its writing system.

Start with the eight practice signs, reveal their transliterations, then try the quiz. Transliteration is a writing convention, not an exact recording of ancient pronunciation.

What does “all signs” mean?

This catalog covers 5,067 encoded signs: 1,072 in the Egyptian Hieroglyphs block and 3,995 in Extended-A. It excludes layout controls and variation sequences and is not a claim to include every historical sign or variant.

Catalog entries show a glyph and Unicode code point, not a complete dictionary of meanings or readings. Only the eight starter signs have curated learning labels here.

The web font supports the original block; Extended-A may require a newer compatible font installed on your device. Empty boxes indicate missing font support, not missing catalog records.
