# Fursa branding

## Name and purpose

- Name: Fursa | فرصة
- Meaning: opportunity.
- Tagline: Your opportunity to improve.
- Purpose: Arabic vocabulary practice and study tools.

## Scope

Rebrand the Arabic study site under `arabic/` only. The personal portfolio is unchanged. This document records the agreed direction; it does not itself change the live site.

## Visual direction

- Retain the existing purple palette and light/dark themes.
- Develop a simple open-doorway symbol representing opportunity, with a subtle F shape.
- Keep the favicon recognizable at small sizes; do not put the full name inside it.
- Prepare an SVG favicon, PNG/ICO alternatives, an Apple touch icon, and a matching wordmark.
- Review the icon and wordmark before applying branding across pages.

## Naming conventions

- Use Fursa as the product name and Fursa | فرصة where the bilingual wordmark fits.
- Use descriptive browser titles such as `Glossary · Fursa`, `Flashcards · Fursa`, and `Quiz · Fursa`.
- Replace legacy product branding such as UAD Study Companion and Arabic Study where it names the product. Preserve useful descriptive text and historical references.
- Update navigation labels, homepage copy, shared footer branding, page descriptions, and social-sharing metadata consistently.
- Retain Caleb Pfohl's creator credit and appropriate unofficial-course and content-rights disclaimers. Do not imply official affiliation.

## Guardrails

- Preserve existing URLs and file paths.
- Preserve accounts, saved progress, storage keys, and Firebase configuration.
- Preserve vocabulary, quizzes, flashcards, audio, and existing functionality.
- Do not rename technical identifiers merely to match the new brand.
- Keep changes limited to branding and necessary asset references.

## Rollout checklist

- [ ] Audit active study pages and shared scripts for legacy branding.
- [ ] Design and review the favicon and wordmark.
- [ ] Add favicon assets and references to active pages.
- [ ] Update shared navigation/footer branding and creator credit presentation.
- [ ] Update homepage branding and introduction.
- [ ] Update individual page titles and visible product references.
- [ ] Update descriptions and social-preview metadata/assets.
- [ ] Check desktop/mobile layouts and light/dark themes.
- [ ] Verify navigation, audio, accounts, and saved progress remain unchanged.
- [ ] Review the implementation PR before merging.

Implementation should follow in a separate reviewable PR after the visual assets are approved.
