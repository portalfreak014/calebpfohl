# Fursa branding

## Name and purpose

- Name: Fursa | فرصة
- Meaning: opportunity.
- Tagline: Your opportunity to improve.
- Purpose: Arabic vocabulary practice and study tools.

## Scope

Rebrand the Arabic study site under `arabic/` only. The personal portfolio is unchanged. This document records the agreed direction; it does not itself change the live site.

## Visual direction

- Retain the existing purple palette and light/dark themes.
- Use an unmistakably open door to represent opportunity, combined with the Arabic letter fa (`ف`), not an English F.
- Keep the favicon recognizable at small sizes; do not put the full name inside it.
- Prepare an SVG favicon, PNG/ICO alternatives, an Apple touch icon, and a matching wordmark.
- Review the final production icon and wordmark before applying branding across pages.

### Selected concept: open door and Arabic fa

The latest concept discussed on November 23, 2026 is the visual direction for further development:

- A straight rectangular doorway frame with a visibly swung-out door panel, clear negative space through the opening, and a small doorknob where size permits.
- A recognizable Arabic `ف` within the opening, retaining its single dot above. Do not distort the letter into a Latin F or sacrifice its readability to create the door.
- The open door must be immediately recognizable rather than reading as an abstract arch.
- Simple flat geometric forms in deep purple and lavender, without gradients or intricate ornament.
- A bilingual wordmark reading `Fursa | فرصة`, with correctly connected Arabic lettering, plus the tagline `Your opportunity to improve.`
- Consistent light- and dark-background icon variants. Simplify fine details for favicon sizes while retaining the open-door silhouette and the letter's dot.

This supersedes the original subtle English-F concept. The generated concept sheet is a design reference, not a production-ready favicon. The image is not embedded or stored in this document; production assets still need to be prepared and reviewed.

## Naming conventions

- Use Fursa as the product name and Fursa | فرصة where the bilingual wordmark fits.
- Use descriptive browser titles such as `Glossary · Fursa`, `Flashcards · Fursa`, and `Quiz · Fursa`.
- Replace legacy product branding such as UAD Study Companion and Arabic Study where it names the product. Preserve useful descriptive text and historical references.
- Update navigation labels, homepage copy, shared footer branding, page descriptions, and social-sharing metadata consistently.
- Retain Caleb Pfohl's creator credit and appropriate unofficial-course and content-rights disclaimers. Do not imply official affiliation.

## Guardrails

- Preserve existing URLs and file paths.
- Preserve accounts, saved progress, storage keys, and Firebase configuration.
- Preserve vocabulary, quizzes, flashcards, audio, and existing functionality.
- Do not rename technical identifiers merely to match the new brand.
- Keep changes limited to branding and necessary asset references.
