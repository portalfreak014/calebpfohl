# Site companion — exploration backlog

Recorded: 2026-11-23.
Status: idea for later exploration; not implemented or scheduled.

## User intent

Explore a friendly on-site companion, inspired by the presence of a helper such as Clippy or a teddy bear. No final character, visual design, personality, or name has been selected. The initial goal is simply for a companion to exist on the site; AI connectivity is a possible later phase, not part of the initial scope.

## Potential phases, subject to future approval

1. Non-AI companion: choose an original character and explore a small, unobtrusive presence with minimal interaction.
2. Optional helper behavior: explore contextual navigation or study hints only after defining useful interactions.
3. Possible AI connection: separately evaluate purpose, privacy, data access, moderation, cost, and backend requirements before selecting a service.

These are exploration directions, not approved implementation requirements. Do not add AI calls, credentials, analytics, or user-data collection merely because this document exists.

## Questions for exploration

- Character: teddy bear, another original mascot, or a different friendly helper?
- Placement and availability across desktop/mobile and focused quiz/flashcard screens?
- What does the first non-AI version do when activated?
- How can users hide it, and should that preference persist?
- How should light/dark themes, keyboard access, screen readers, and reduced motion work?

## Guardrails for any future proposal

- Do not obscure vocabulary, quiz choices, navigation, or controls.
- Keep studying usable without the companion or an account.
- Avoid unsolicited speech or distracting animations.
- Preserve the Fursa visual direction and unofficial-project notices.
- Treat Clippy as inspiration for a helper concept, not a request to copy its artwork or identity.
- Require separate approval for design, implementation, and any AI/data integration.

## Current action

Documentation only. No mascot artwork, site component, AI integration, or behavior is introduced by this backlog entry. It is stored in the repository; docs viewer discovery requires adding it to the documentation manifest in a separate update.
